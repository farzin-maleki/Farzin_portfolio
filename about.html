About

Who I am

I'm a frontend developer who hand-codes modern, production-ready websites with HTML, CSS and JavaScript. What started as curiosity about how the web works turned into a focus on shipping real things for real people.

I've delivered live client projects end to end — from layout and responsive design to contact forms, booking systems and payment flows. I care about clean code, fast load times and interfaces that feel effortless on every screen.

Whether it's a company looking for a reliable developer or a client who needs a site that actually converts, I bring attention to detail and a get-it-shipped mindset.

My approach

Good websites start with understanding.

01 / Understand

Start with the people using the website: what they need, what the business offers, and what a successful visit looks like.

02 / Shape

Organise the content and page structure, then work through a responsive layout with clear navigation and useful calls to action.

03 / Build

Turn the design into readable code and connect the forms, booking systems or other services the project needs.

04 / Refine

Check the experience across screen sizes, work through the details, and get the finished website ready to go live.
